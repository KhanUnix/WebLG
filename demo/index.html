WebLG

From glass to interface.

WebLG pairs an optical engine with native HTML components. Buttons, switches, sliders, tabs and menus keep their interactions and accessibility.

In the studio

  • Drag the panel by its handle, or use the arrow keys.
  • Select a component, then adjust its material.
  • Hold Space outside form controls to compare.
  • Try a texture or your own image. Your image stays in your browser.

In your project

  • A standalone WebGL engine, a Three.js adapter and a framework-free UI API.
  • The background can be an image, a canvas or a scene rendered to a texture.
  • Glass layers are composed on the GPU while HTML text stays sharp.
  • Motion, contrast and transparency preferences are respected.

Your first component

The web rendering follows Apple’s material and interaction principles. It is built with WebLG and has no Apple framework dependencies.
